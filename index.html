Our History

PAKISTAN FF GUILD was founded in 2021 by a group of passionate Free Fire players who wanted to create a disciplined and competitive gaming community. What started as a small squad of 5 friends has now grown into a powerhouse of 50+ active warriors. Within our first year, we broke into the Top 100 PK Leaderboard and have maintained that position for 6 consecutive seasons.

Our guild has achieved an incredible 32-win streak in Guild Wars, with over 15 players consistently scoring 200+ glory points per war. We have trained fivthy of players who have gone on to become elite Rushers and Survivors.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: To represent Pakistan on the global Free Fire esports stage. We aim to train the next generation of FF pros through structured coaching, daily practice sessions, and a supportive community. We believe in discipline, respect, and continuous improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about joining and guild operations

What rank is required to join?
Your BR/CS rank must be Elite, Heroic, Master, or Grandmaster. We welcome all dedicated players who are willing to learn and improve. If you're below Elite, you can still apply for our training program.
How can I become a Rusher?
To earn the Rusher role, you must score 150+ glory points in a guild war trial match. Our elders will evaluate your performance including kills, assists, and overall game sense. After consistent performance, you'll be officially assigned the Rusher role.
Is there any membership fee?
No fees at all! PAKISTAN FF GUILD is 100% free. We only require dedication, regular guild war attendance (minimum 3 days/week), and that you change your game name to include the Pᴀᴋɪꜱᴛᴀɴ FF prefix.
What are the guild war timings?
Guild Wars run daily from 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM Pakistan Time (PKT). Active attendance is required for at least 3 days per week. Top performers are recognized weekly.
How do I contact the guild leaders?
You can reach us via WhatsApp at 0344 7041131, or use the contact form above. You can also directly message any of our leaders through the WhatsApp floating button on the bottom right of this page.
Do you host tournaments?
Yes! We host monthly internal tournaments with prizes including Google Play Gift Cards, Free Fire Diamonds, and exclusive in-game items. Top guild members also get chances to represent us in external competitive events.
What is the anti-cheat policy?
We have a zero-tolerance policy for hacking, cheating, or using any third-party tools. Any member found violating this rule will be permanently banned and reported to Garena. We pride ourselves on fair play.
